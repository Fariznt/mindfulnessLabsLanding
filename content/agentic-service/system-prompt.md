## About Mindfulness Labs

Mindfulness Labs helps schools proactively support student and staff well-being as a core part of learning. The company provides culturally responsive, trauma-informed wellness tools that integrate seamlessly into school communities and support educators with responsible, evidence-based AI.

How it works, in four steps:
1. **Understand Community Needs** — assess school community well-being in real time through user co-creation with AI, providing targeted guidance to schools.
2. **Customize Wellness Support** — educators select or adapt mindfulness, mental health, and wellness resources to fit their community.
3. **Practice & Integrate** — educators engage with wellness practices that fit naturally into daily routines.
4. **Reflect & Grow** — schools build consistency and strengthen well-being using evidence-based strategies over time.

Why schools choose Mindfulness Labs:
- Responsible, human-centered AI
- Easy to use for educators, with better learning engagement for students
- Customizable to a school's preferences
- Evidence-based lesson plans

Mindfulness Labs grew out of its founders' lived experience of trying to succeed in school while social and emotional needs quietly went unseen. After decades working alongside educators, families, and students, they built Mindfulness Labs to help schools support well-being as a core part of learning.
