## About Mindfulness Labs

Mindfulness Labs is a P-12 mental health and well-being platform built by Effective to Great Education (ETGE). It helps schools make well-being foundational to learning through evidence-based supports for mental health, wellness, and school climate. The platform is designed for educators, school mental health teams, and school leadership, and serves the whole school community — students, staff, and families — from pre-K through 12th grade.

Mindfulness Labs has been piloted with 250+ students, 30+ educators, and 15+ school mental health professionals across six school communities, developed in partnership with educators, families, students, and university researchers.

### What the platform includes

Six core capability areas:
- **School Wellness Management** — a prevention and management system that assesses school community well-being in real time through co-creation, giving targeted guidance in a culturally responsive, trauma-informed way inclusive of Black, Brown, and Indigenous experience.
- **Measuring School Community Well-Being** — monitoring and screening tools that track feelings of students, staff, and families, plus truancy, behavior growth, and academic performance, giving leaders real-time insight.
- **Learning Mental Health and Well-Being** — curriculum and content on mindfulness, mental health, basic neuroscience, and healthy foods, with activities in mindfulness, restorative justice, social-emotional learning, and MTSS.
- **Engaging Restorative Justice** — an anti-bullying and violence prevention framework and practices for safer, more supportive communities.
- **Engaging Mental and Physical Well-Being** — holistic wellness practices including yoga, fitness, breathing, nutrition, affirmation, meditation, connection with nature, and healthy environments for students and educators.
- **Engaging Emotional Regulation** — skill-building in emotional literacy, mindfulness, self-awareness, social awareness, relationships, and decision-making.

The platform also includes AI-supported lessons and resources that integrate well-being into everyday learning, and educator training and professional development focused on mental health.

Cultural responsiveness is core to the platform: deeply inclusive mental health resources designed to support healthy self-identity and affirm historically marginalized communities, including Black, Brown, and Indigenous students and families.

### Why schools choose Mindfulness Labs

Four core benefits:
- **Student Well-Being** — evidence-based wellness content, emotional regulation tools, and culturally responsive resources that help students manage stress, build self-awareness, and stay engaged in learning.
- **Educator Support** — AI-supported lesson content, professional development on mental health, and tools to support educators' own well-being.
- **School Climate Insights** — real-time data and trends on well-being across students, staff, and families, helping leaders decide where to focus support.
- **MTSS and PBIS Alignment** — designed to align with Multi-Tiered System of Supports and Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports, so schools can adopt it without rebuilding existing systems.

The platform is built on evidence-based practices developed in partnership with university researchers, grounded in trauma-informed care and culturally responsive frameworks.

### Getting started

- Schools get started by scheduling a 30-minute school leadership demo through the contact form on this site (follow-up within 2–3 business days).
- Works with public, private, and charter schools, pre-K through 12th grade.
- Implementation is customized to each school's needs and can integrate into classroom routines, advisory periods, school-wide wellness programming, or existing MTSS/PBIS frameworks — the team works with school leadership to determine the right approach.
- Educator training and support are built into the platform, so it fits into existing school structures without requiring teachers to overhaul their approach.
